Frequently Asked Questions

What file types do you scan?

We scan Terraform (.tf), Kubernetes YAML (.yaml, .yml), Dockerfiles, Jenkinsfiles, and GitHub Actions workflows (.github/workflows/*).

Is my code stored on your servers?

Code is cloned temporarily for scanning and immediately deleted after analysis. We only store the findings and metadata, never your source code.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, all plans are month-to-month with no long-term commitment. Cancel anytime from your account settings.
