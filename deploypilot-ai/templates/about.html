About InfraAudit AI

Making infrastructure security accessible to every developer

Our Mission

Infrastructure misconfigurations are the #1 cause of cloud security breaches. Yet most teams don't have dedicated security engineers reviewing every Terraform file, Kubernetes manifest, or CI/CD pipeline. InfraAudit AI bridges that gap — providing instant, actionable security feedback that helps developers ship secure infrastructure from day one.

Our Story

Founded in 2026, DeployPilot AI started with a simple observation: developers are writing more infrastructure code than ever, but security tooling hasn't kept up. Traditional scanners are slow, noisy, and disconnected from the developer workflow.

We built InfraAudit AI to be different — fast, developer-friendly, and focused on actionable findings rather than overwhelming alerts. Every finding comes with a clear explanation and a specific fix, mapped to industry compliance frameworks.
